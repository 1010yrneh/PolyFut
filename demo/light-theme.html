Why can this take a while?

  • Analysis runs entirely on your computer — every frame is decoded and scanned locally, not in the cloud.
  • Long matches are processed in 30-second chunks; a 90-minute game can mean ~180+ chunks.
  • The engine looks for players and the ball on thousands of sampled frames so distant touches are not missed.
  • Slow progress is expected, not a freeze. Keep your laptop plugged in.

The one rule that shapes everything: the brand neon measures 1.33:1 against white, where 4.5:1 is the minimum for text. It cannot be a text colour on a light theme. So it keeps the job it is genuinely good at — a solid fill behind a dark label, where it measures 14:1 — and a deeper green does the reading work. That is why the primary "go" buttons are still neon while links, labels and figures are #0b7a42.

Also changed from the dark theme, per the brief: gradients removed, corners softened to 6px, Anton kept only for large figures where a condensed face earns its place, and more whitespace throughout.
